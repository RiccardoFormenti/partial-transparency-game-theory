Figures. figures.py draws them all; each figure is saved here as figure_<X>.pdf and .png.

Data: two sweeps of the (t, α) grid, under results/partial_transparency/. Both use the zoo
body8 (floor side) and both signal families (behavioral and syntactic). They also share the
transparency grid t = 0, 0.01, ..., 1. Each grid point's game is solved for its exact Nash
equilibria and for where the replicator dynamics end, starting from seeded random populations
(seed 20260514).
  body8/          α ∈ {0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.5, 0.6, 0.8, 1}, the original paper's cautions.
                  1000 replicator starts per game.
  alpha_0.5-1/    α ∈ {0.5, 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9, 1}, a finer α step of 0.1.
                  100 replicator starts per game, the first 100 of the 1000 above.

Figure A (figure_A.pdf, figure_A.png)
  x: transparency t, from 0 to 1.
  y: maximum equilibrium cooperation, Pr[(C, C)] in the most cooperative equilibrium, averaged
     over the caution values α ∈ {0.5, 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9, 1}.
  The dotted line is the minimum equilibrium cooperation, which is 0 at every t.
  Every replicator dynamics run converges to the maximum-cooperation equilibrium, at every
  (t, α) pair. The green band, which the max equilibrium line runs inside, shows this.
  Data: behavioral family, results/partial_transparency/alpha_0.5-1/.

Figure B (figure_B.pdf, figure_B.png)
  x: caution α ∈ {0.5, 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9, 1}.
  y: critical transparency t*, the lowest grid t from which on the replicator dynamics
     converge to full cooperation. Below it they converge to full defection, with a single
     switch in both families. In both families t* is also the lowest t at which a fully
     cooperative equilibrium exists.
  Solid line with points: behavioral family, t* = 0.11, 0.24, 0.37, 0.5, 0.67, 1.
  Dashed, lighter line: syntactic family, t* = 0.07, 0.2, 0.37, 0.57, 0.79, 1.
  Above a curve the population cooperates; below it, it defects. Each switch lies between t*
  and t* - 0.01, the grid step. t* rises with caution in both families. The syntactic family
  needs less transparency at α ≤ 0.6 and more at α ≥ 0.8. The two agree at α = 0.7 and at
  α = 1, where only full transparency sustains cooperation. The behavioral points are colored
  by α as in figure C.
  In the syntactic family at α = 0.8 and 0.9, a partly cooperative equilibrium (Pr[(C, C)] =
  11/18) exists somewhat below t* (from t = 0.49 and 0.66). The replicator dynamics do not
  reach it and still converge to defection there.
  Data: both families, results/partial_transparency/alpha_0.5-1/.

Figure C (figure_C.pdf, figure_C.png)
  Six panels, one per α ∈ {0.5, 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9, 1}.
  x: transparency t, from 0 to 1.
  y: cooperation the replicator dynamics converge to at that α, a solid line in the α's
     viridis color. It equals the maximum equilibrium cooperation (figure A).
  Each α switches once, at its t* (figure B, labeled in each panel), from full defection to
  full cooperation. The switch is dashed, and a dot marks where cooperation starts.
  Averaging these six switches gives figure A's six steps of 1/6.
  Data: behavioral family, results/partial_transparency/alpha_0.5-1/.

Figure D (figure_D.pdf, figure_D.png)
  Figures C and B side by side: (a) is figure C, (b) is figure B, drawn by the same code.

Figure E (figure_E.pdf, figure_E.png)
  A phase diagram of the Nash components over transparency and caution.
  x: transparency t, from 0 to 1.
  y: caution α, from 0.5 to 1.
  Every component has a single cooperation level Pr[(C, C)], and only three sets occur. Two
  dark gray boundaries split the plane into three regions. Each region is shaded in a light
  tint: blue and red from the RdYlBu palette (#4575b4, #d73027), and viridis yellow
  (#fde725). Within 0.2 in of a boundary or of the frame the tint softly lightens, to 40% of
  its strength at the line. The blue and red regions are labeled inside. The yellow band is
  too narrow for its label, which sits just above it with a short line pointing in. The
  labels are dark gray, like the other figures' annotations. Each names what the region adds
  to the one before:
    blue    "defection only"      full defection (0) only.
    yellow  "+ full cooperation"  full defection (0) and full cooperation (1).
    red     "+ partial (11/18)"   full defection (0), full cooperation (1), and partial
                                  cooperation (11/18).
  Full defection is an equilibrium everywhere. As t grows, each α gains full cooperation at
  t* (the left boundary, the same as figure B) and the partial component at t** (the right
  boundary):
    α     0.5   0.6   0.7   0.8   0.9   1
    t*    0.11  0.24  0.37  0.5   0.67  1
    t**   0.28  0.39  0.5   0.69  0.88  1
  The dots are these sampled values. The boundaries connect them with straight lines; between
  the six α the boundaries are not computed. Each switch lies between the dot's t and the grid
  t 0.01 below it. At α = 1 both components appear only at t = 1, where the boundaries meet.
  The replicator dynamics converge to full cooperation right of t* (yellow and red) and to
  defection left of it.
  All red points play one game, the full-transparency game: there the signal is sharp
  enough that every bot acts as at full transparency. The replicator dynamics never select
  the partial component.
  Data: behavioral family, results/partial_transparency/alpha_0.5-1/.
