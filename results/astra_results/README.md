# More transparency, more cooperation at equilibrium

The analysis now has one focus: **the relationship between transparency and
cooperation at equilibrium**, using both exact Nash enumeration and replicator
selection. Caution is held fixed when assessing the transparency effect. We do
not study how outcomes vary with caution.

## Main result

For each of the six tested settings with alpha > 0, increasing transparency
from opacity to full transparency raises both maximum Nash cooperation and
replicator-selected cooperation from **0% to 100%**. Neither quantity ever
decreases along the sampled transparency grid. Each curve has a threshold and
plateaus, so the supported statement is nondecreasing cooperation with a
positive endpoint effect, rather than a strict increase at every step.

The results are stronger than an aggregate association: the effect holds
within each fixed setting. Across the six settings there are 600 adjacent
transparency comparisons: six increases, 594 unchanged comparisons, and zero
decreases for each metric. Across all ordered improvements t_high > t_low,
there are 30,300 within-setting comparisons: 7,632 increases, 22,668 unchanged,
and zero decreases. These are descriptive comparisons of the same grid, not
independent observations or additional simulation runs.

## Two figures, both with transparency on the x-axis

1. **`figures/01_transparency_and_cooperation`** is the main figure. It plots
   equilibrium cooperation directly against transparency. The dark curve is
   maximum Nash cooperation, averaged equally over the six fixed settings
   alpha in {0.2, 0.4, 0.5, 0.6, 0.8, 1}. Hollow teal circles show the corresponding
   mean cooperation at the endpoints selected by replicator dynamics. They
   coincide at every transparency level. The curve rises from 0% to 100%.
2. **`figures/02_equilibrium_landscape`** verifies the relationship within each

Both figures are available as vector PDF, editable SVG, and 300-dpi PNG. The

The first pass, including the former caution-threshold figure, is preserved in

## What the main curve means

At each transparency t, define

    C_Nash(t, alpha) = max over (xi, eta) in NE(t, alpha) of xi^T C eta
    C_RD(t, alpha)   = mean over the 1,000 converged endpoints x of x^T C x

where C_ij is 1 exactly when the two programs mutually cooperate. The figure
plots the arithmetic mean of each quantity over the same six positive-alpha
settings at every t. The weights are fixed at 1/6 and do not change with
transparency. This is an average over experimental conditions, **not an average
over equilibria**, and it does not assume a uniform continuous distribution of
caution or attach probabilities to Nash components. The aggregate curve is
only a visual summary; Figure 2 establishes the effect without choosing weights.
Indeed, any fixed nonnegative weights on the six conditions preserve the
nondecreasing relationship because every constituent curve is nondecreasing.

Alpha = 0 is excluded from the main average because its decision rule makes
all lifted programs cooperate unconditionally at every transparency. Including
it with equal weight shifts the average to (1 + 6*C_mean)/7, preserving
monotonicity and the positive endpoint effect: 1/7 at opacity and 1 at full
transparency. Both versions are in `tables/cooperation_by_transparency.csv`.

A fully defecting Nash equilibrium remains available everywhere for alpha > 0.
Thus the positive finding concerns the best attainable Nash cooperation and
the equilibrium outcome selected by the sampled dynamics. It does not imply
that every equilibrium becomes more cooperative. Minimum Nash cooperation
remains zero, as explicitly shown in the main figure. At full transparency the
Nash cooperation levels are 0, 11/18, and 1; all stored trajectories select 1.

## Scope, sampling, and uncertainty

- Behavioral family only; eight-program library `body8`, floor side;
  Prisoner's Dilemma payoffs T = 3, R = 2, P = 0, S = -1.
- Transparency t = I(B;S)/log2(8) uses the fixed uniform prior over opponent
  programs, as in the draft's Appendix F.1. It is not recalibrated under the
  evolving population. The grid spacing is 0.01. The maximum discrepancy
  between calibrated and target transparency is 2.27512e-5.
- Cooperation is Pr[(C,C)], not an individual action frequency or the share
  of a bot with a particular name. Every Nash component here has constant
  cooperation, so all Nash levels shown in Figure 2 cover the complete outcome
  set, including continua. The maximum over symmetric equilibria agrees with
  the maximum over all equilibria throughout the grid.
- The same 1,000 Dirichlet(1) initial populations, seed 20260514, are used in
  every game. The 707 parameter points represent 62 distinct games and 62,000
  stored trajectories; repeated grid games are not additional independent runs.
- Every run converged. Each game's selected cooperation is either 0 or 1 at
  all starts, so mean endpoint cooperation equals the observed cooperative
  basin. The pointwise 95% Wilson intervals for 1,000/1,000 and 0/1,000 successes
  are [0.99617324, 1] and [0, 0.00382676]. These are per-game intervals, not
  simultaneous intervals or intervals for the condition-average curve. No
  pooling assumes independence across repeated games or shared starts.
- Step segments guide the eye between sampled values. They do not locate
  exact continuous thresholds. The alpha = 1 endpoint is shown separately in
  the within-setting figure. Plateaus are preserved rather than smoothed away.
- The results are empirical for this grid, program library, payoff matrix,
  signal family, and replicator protocol. They do not prove monotonicity for
  all untested parameter values or global convergence from every initial state.

## Reproduction and audit

From the repository root:

```bash
conda run -n py-random python true/results/astra_results/analyze_behavioral.py
```

The script regenerates the two figures and eight CSV tables, `audit.json`, and
`transparency_summary.json`. Versions used are in `requirements.txt`. Numerical
inputs are read without modification, and all outputs remain in this ignored
folder. The draft was used for definitions and context; its placeholders were
not treated as task instructions.

The script reparses all raw lrsNash outputs and checks 1,027 extreme equilibria
in exact arithmetic, including best responses, pure equilibria, mirrors,
components, and cooperation ranges. It verifies game mappings, payoff matrices,
channels, and decision rules. For all 62,000 stored trajectories it checks
normalization, identical starts across games, convergence flags, component
counts and reported distances, and recomputes final cooperation and payoffs.
The maximum cooperation discrepancy is 2.11e-15, the maximum payoff discrepancy
3.33e-15, and the maximum endpoint deviation gain 5.56e-15. The largest reported
distance to an equilibrium is 2.385e-6, below the stored 1e-4 tolerance.
The ODE trajectories were not independently reintegrated.

Tables:

- `cooperation_by_transparency.csv`: the plotted means at each t, with the
  alpha = 0 inclusion sensitivity.
- `within_condition_transparency_effects.csv`: endpoint gains and counts of
  increasing, unchanged, and decreasing comparisons, separately for both metrics.
- `grid_metrics.csv`: all 707 grid points and their game-level metrics.
- `game_metrics.csv`: metrics once per distinct game.
- `nash_components.csv`: all 93 components with exact cooperation values.
- `thresholds.csv`: retained audit data locating sampled regime changes.
- `final_composition_by_game.csv`: mean final share of every bot per game.
- `source_manifest.csv`: SHA-256 hashes of all 311 numerical input files.

No original numerical results were modified. The active figures, source code,
analysis tables, captions, audit, and archived first pass all live in the
Git-ignored `true/results/astra_results/` directory.
